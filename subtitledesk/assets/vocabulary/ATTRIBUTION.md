# Vocabulary datasets

Source: https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj

- Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2, version 1.0, by Octanove Labs:
  `octanove-vocabulary-profile-c1c2-1.0.csv`. Licensed under Creative Commons
  Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
- The CEFR-J Wordlist, version 1.5, compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University
  of Foreign Studies, copyright Tono Laboratory at TUFS:
  `cefrj-vocabulary-profile-1.5.csv`. Research and commercial use without charge
  is permitted with proper citation. Used to exclude A1-B2 homographs and
  their regular inflections. Dataset terms: https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj#terms-of-use.

Original CSV files are redistributed without edits. Revision and SHA-256 hashes
are recorded in `provenance.json`. Software lookup tables generated at runtime
split spelling variants, merge repeated terms and exclude basic-level overlaps.
CEFR annotations in exported cards retain the Octanove attribution and CC BY-SA
4.0 notice; subtitle excerpts retain their original rights.

Coverage is finite. A headword's level is an estimate, not a judgment of its
meaning in a particular sentence. Unknown words are excluded. No guarantee of
complete advanced vocabulary coverage, translation, or dictionary definitions.
